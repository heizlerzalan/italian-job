Welcome to Treviso

The elegance of water, the whisper of history" Treviso is not just a stopover; it is a destination to be breathed in slowly. Between canals reflecting frescoed palaces and the lively rhythm of its squares, our city welcomes you with a discreet elegance and authentic hospitality. Whether you are an art lover, a history buff, or a seeker of true Italian flavors, you have found the perfect place to discover the soul of the Marca.

Who We Are

Your compass in the labyrinth of canals. We are a collective of enthusiasts, storytellers, and local insiders who have come together to assemble the pieces of the mosaic that is Treviso. Our goal is simple: to guide you beyond the typical tourist paths.
We aren't a traditional, rigid guidebook. Instead, consider us a travel companion sharing the secrets of the Urbs Picta, explaining the resilience of our ancient walls, and revealing where the magic of the world’s most famous dessert truly began. We believe in the beauty of details and in celebrating a territory that deserves to be known in every facet.

What to Do in Treviso: An Experience for Every Desire

In Treviso, every corner invites a different discovery. Here is how you can experience the city with us:
Lose Yourself in Art and History: Wander beneath medieval porticos to admire the painted facades that turn the city center into an open-air museum. From the majesty of Piazza dei Signori to the quiet spirituality of the Cathedral, every step is a journey into the past.
Follow the Rhythm of the Waters: Enjoy a stroll along the Buranelli Canal or the Restera path along the Sile River. You can rent a bike or simply walk where the rivers intersect, discovering ancient watermills and views that look like they were plucked from a Renaissance painting.
Live the Culinary Tradition: You cannot say you’ve been to Treviso without tasting the original Tiramisù in the very city where it was born. Stop by the stalls of the picturesque Fish Market (La Pescheria) to feel the commercial heartbeat of the city and end your day with a classic aperitivo in a historic tavern.
Explore the 16th-Century Walls: Head up to the ramparts for a morning jog or an afternoon picnic. The Venetian walls are not just a monument; they are the "green lung" of the city where locals meet to relax and enjoy the view.
